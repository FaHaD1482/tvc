Facilities · Kajang, Selangor

The warehouse behind both lines.

Every carton of apparel and every crate of produce passes through our facility at Reko Sentral, Kajang. It is where goods are received, checked, held and prepared for wholesale.

Get directions Talk to us

Inside the facility

Goods in, goods out. Nothing sits uncounted.

Containers arrive from the port and consignments arrive from growers. Everything is received against paperwork, checked, and put away to its slot in the racks. When a wholesale order comes in, stock is picked, packed and dispatched to Port Dickson.

  • One holding point for both trading lines
  • Import consignments cleared and received here
  • Produce moved quickly, stock rotated first-in-first-out

What happens here

Four jobs, done in sequence.

01

Receiving & import handling

Imported consignments arrive from the port; produce arrives from growers. Every delivery is checked against its paperwork before it is put away.

02

Inspection & QC hold

Garments are inspected for finishing and counts; produce is checked for freshness and condition before stock is accepted.

03

Sorting & stock control

Goods are sorted by style, size and consignment, and held in racks. Produce rotates first-in-first-out so nothing waits too long.

04

Dispatch & distribution

Wholesale orders are picked and packed here, then moved to the Port Dickson showroom for sale to retail buyers.
