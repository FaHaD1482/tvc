Sourcing approach

Three steps from grower to buyer.

  1. 01

    Grower selection

    We buy from growers who can deliver consistent quality in volume, season after season.

  2. 02

    Quality check

    Deliveries are checked for freshness and condition at the Kajang warehouse before being accepted into stock.

  3. 03

    Wholesale supply

    Stock rotates first-in-first-out and moves quickly to the Port Dickson showroom for retail buyers.

Reliable supply

One chain, kept tight.

Produce is the faster game: what arrives today should be with buyers before the week is out. Our warehouse runs the vegetable line on quick rotation while the apparel line holds stock on the racks beside it.
