Culture · Principles

How our leadership guides daily trade.

Operating across borders requires accountability at every link—from international factory audits to wholesale warehouse distribution.

Audited Production

We contract strictly with overseas factories certified under BSCI, WRAP, Sedex, and ISO, ensuring ethical labor and structural compliance.

Floor-Level Presence

Our directors and managers maintain daily oversight on the warehouse floor at Kajang, personally inspecting incoming shipments and sorting.

Fair Employment

Transparent human resources, safe operational facilities, fair wages, and continuous on-the-job training for all warehouse and office staff.

Wholesale Reliability
