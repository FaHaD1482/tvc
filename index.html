How We Operate

Two routes, the same four stops.

  1. 01

    Sourcing / Production

    Garments are cut, sewn and quality-checked at a certified manufacturing partner; produce is sourced fresh from growers.

  2. 02

    Import & Warehousing

    Stock is received and held at the Kajang warehouse, the company's central holding point for both trading lines.

  3. 03

    Wholesale Sale

    Goods move to the Port Dickson showroom, where they're sold on to Malaysian retail buyers.

  4. 04

    Administration

    Licensing, management and company affairs are run from the head office in Cheras, Kuala Lumpur.
